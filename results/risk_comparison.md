## Comparative Assessment

ChatGPT and Gemini both handled the limitations of the input dataset well. Each identified observable market volatility from the large annual market-index movements, while acknowledging that credit risk and liquidity risk require additional evidence.

ChatGPT was more explicit in quantifying market changes and in maintaining a final list of what was supported by the data versus what depended on assumptions. Gemini offered somewhat more specific operational mitigation actions, including lower loan-to-value limits, increased loan-loss reserves, high-quality liquid assets, and pre-committed backup credit lines.

Neither model should be treated as a substitute for a financial risk manager. The analysis must be supplemented with direct credit measures, liquidity measures, verified data sources, and professional human review before use in real decisions.
